FR Rapor Bridge — Kurulum
=========================

1) POS bilgisayarinda (Windows, Yonetici):
   - branch-agent klasorunu kopyalayin
   - PowerShell (Yonetici) acin:
     cd ...\branch-agent
     powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1
   - Postgres host/port/db/user/sifre sorun (genelde 127.0.0.1, 5432, fastrest, begum)
   - Ekranda XXX-XXX esleme kodu cikar
     Ayrica: C:\ProgramData\FrRaporBridge\PAIR-CODE.txt
     Masaustu: FR-RAPOR-KOD.txt

2) FR Rapor (telefon / web):
   - Sube Ekle > "Kod ile bagla"
   - Sube adi + kodu yazin > Bagla

3) Otomatik yeniden baslatma:
   - Windows Service: FrRaporBridge (baslangicta acilir)
   - Crash olursa ~1 sn icinde yeniden baslar (sc failure)
   - run.cmd dongusu ek koruma
   - Her dakika watchdog servisi kontrol eder

Kaldirma: uninstall.ps1 (Yonetici)
Log: C:\ProgramData\FrRaporBridge\bridge.log
